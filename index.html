Why is RoofWrap a better option than a blue tarp?

A blue tarp is a 90-day product on a problem that lasts a year or more. It flaps, tears, and blows off in the next storm, and every nail used to hold it puts another hole in your roof. RoofWrap is a 12 mil stretch-wrap membrane tensioned drum-tight and mechanically attached into one continuous watertight surface with zero penetrations, wind tested past 150 mph and rated for up to 24 months. It looks better, lasts longer, and keeps your home dry until the permanent roof is done.

How fast can you wrap my roof?

In most cases within hours to a couple of days of your call. RoofWrap installs fast so your home is protected before the next band of rain, not after it.

Does RoofWrap put holes in my roof like a tarp?

No. RoofWrap is tensioned and sealed to the structure with zero penetrations. No nails through your decking, no sandbags, no new leaks.

Can RoofWrap protect a tile, slate, or custom roof while I wait on replacement?

Yes. Tile, slate, and custom roofing often run on long lead times of many months. RoofWrap seals the roof so your home stays completely dry until the matching tile or slate arrives and the permanent roof can be installed, so you never have to rush a mismatched substitute.

Will RoofWrap help with my insurance claim?

Emergency roof protection is a standard mitigation expense on most homeowner claims. We document the damage and the installation with photos so you have what your adjuster needs, and the wrap can be cut for inspection and re-sealed.

How long does a RoofWrap last?

The membrane is rated for up to 24 months of UV exposure and has been wind tested past 150 mph. Most homeowners stay protected from the day of the loss to the day the permanent roof is finished.
